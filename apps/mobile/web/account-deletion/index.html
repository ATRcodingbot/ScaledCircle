Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your ScaledCircle account and associated personal data without installing or signing in to the app.

Request deletion by email

  1. Email support@scaledcircle.com from the email address associated with your ScaledCircle account. Use the subject ScaledCircle account deletion request.
  2. State that you want to delete your ScaledCircle account and associated personal data. Identify whether it is a Business or Scaler account. If you cannot access that email address, explain this to Support so we can verify ownership.
  3. We verify your identity and review any unresolved financial or Business workspace obligations before processing the request. We will explain any steps or retention requirements that apply. Do not send passwords, complete payment-card or banking details, or identity documents in the initial email.
Request account deletion

Delete through the app

Sign in and open your Business Account or Scaler Profile, then choose Delete Account. Review any obligations, verify your sign-in, type the requested confirmation, and confirm deletion. Opening that screen does not delete your account.

What is removed

The account-closure process removes your sign-in and personal profile, revokes sessions, removes personal discovery and market preferences, active tracking-session records, notification preferences and registered device tokens, and removes your notifications and team membership access. A minimal deleted-account marker remains to preserve record integrity.

What may be retained

Required financial and Wallet ledgers, compensation and referral records, work and review evidence, legal-consent records and security/audit history may be retained for accounting, disputes, fraud prevention and legal obligations. Deleting an individual login does not automatically delete a Business workspace, its customer records, communication history or workspace-owned integrations. These records can involve other people or continuing Business obligations.

There is no single fixed automatic expiry for retained Business communication or audit history. Records are retained while needed for their stated purpose or required obligations. Support will review requests for those records separately, including ownership and applicable retention requirements. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Delete specific data without closing your account

Email support@scaledcircle.com with the subject ScaledCircle personal data deletion request and identify the information you want removed. This includes requests about saved Gmail-derived data. We verify ownership and review required retention before removing records. Disconnecting a mailbox stops new access but does not itself delete saved conversation history.
