A workflow example, not a case study

More roofing work
in Baltimore County.

A local contractor has a goal. Here’s how the pieces connect—without assuming leads, revenue or success.

  1. Choose the neighborhoodsUse the Business’s service area to shape a campaign.
  2. Set the work in motionA Scaler accepts, performs the work and records a tracked route.
  3. Connect the responseResidents can respond through configured QR codes, landing pages or tracking paths. Recorded leads and results retain their campaign source.
  4. Decide the next moveUse available execution and response evidence to refine the next campaign.
