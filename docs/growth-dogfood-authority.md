# Staging Growth Agents

The private ScaledCircle dogfood surface uses the existing agent observations,
CRM projections, draft actions and approval records. It is available only to the
verified, enabled Admin bound by the private staging deployment configuration.
It is not an arbitrary Business automation endpoint.

## Current scope

Lead Generator and Workforce Recruiter review a bounded pool of six official
organization sources. This initial pool is not a general web crawler or paid
enrichment integration. The server checks source content before qualifying an
organization or retaining a published contact path. An organization partner is
never represented as an individual worker. Interest, availability and conversion
remain unknown until supported by evidence.

The daily research run creates deterministic observations, prospects, CRM
projections, draft actions, reports and in-app notifications. Failed sources are
reported as unavailable. Reviewed drafts are never sent by this implementation.
The existing external-action kill switch remains enabled; research can also be
paused independently. Social Manager, Ad Manager and Business Assistant retain
their existing authorities and display their lack of new approved input honestly.

Reports describe the stored research inventory. They do not infer signups,
replies, customers or revenue. Communication preferences control important,
daily and weekly report email generation and are checked again before delivery.
Deterministic job IDs prevent duplicate enqueueing; provider acceptance is not
proof of inbox delivery.

Network learning is source-ready only. Its input contract allows enumerated
coarse dimensions and verified outcomes, suppresses small cohorts, and excludes
private contact and conversation data. No cross-Business learning outcome is
claimed without sufficient evidence. Business-specific evidence takes priority.

## Availability

Starter, Growth and Scale remain ordinary purchase choices. New Managed Growth,
Business Assistant, Lead Generation Research, Growth Department and postcard
purchases require a current server-only private-product invitation. Existing
membership, cancellation and historical order access are preserved. No private
invitation or entitlement is created by this changeset.

The older deployed staging billing implementation uses a compatibility adapter
that adds only this purchase boundary to its three existing handlers. Its
archived provider logic and secret bindings are retained; it must not be replaced
by the newer shared billing implementation as part of this staging task. The
adapter fails closed outside staging.

## Verification and promotion

Unit, Flutter, Firestore emulator and Rules tests cover source validation,
deduplication, tenant isolation, Supervisor pause, draft-only decisions, email
preferences, private purchase restrictions and preserved existing history.
Private deployment evidence records the exact selectors, archived runtime hash,
source SHA and resulting revisions. No Rules change or production deployment is
part of this release. Referral payout certification remains a separate attended
provider gate; research does not create any financial events.
