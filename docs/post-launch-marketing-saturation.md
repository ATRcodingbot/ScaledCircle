# Marketing Saturation / Network Intelligence

Status: Founder-approved post-launch queue; documentation only. Do not implement during the launch freeze.

Before a Business launches local marketing, offer a warning when authoritative aggregate evidence indicates unusually heavy recent activity. Potential signals are coarse area, recency, marketing touch count, service category, same-category repetition, nearby lower-saturation alternatives, and verified campaign outcomes. Distinguish platform-observed activity from total market activity; do not fabricate market-wide coverage.

Example concept (illustrative, not a real observation): “High recent marketing activity: 8 ScaledCircle marketing touches in this area during the last 7 days. HVAC 3; Roofing 2; Remodeling 2; Other 1. Consider another nearby territory or wait several days.”

Never disclose another Business's identity, spend, customer list, exact campaign, route, or proprietary strategy. Before implementation, define aggregation thresholds, time/geography buckets, sparse-category suppression and repeated-query safeguards so aggregates cannot identify individual Businesses. No cross-tenant raw data access.

Relationship to existing products: Property Intelligence answers where; Weather Intelligence answers when; Marketing Saturation helps assess whether to market here now. Recommendations remain advisory and preserve each Business's geography authority.
