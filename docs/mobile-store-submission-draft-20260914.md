## App description draft

ScaledCircle brings your Business workspace and Scaler work into one app.

Business users can manage their existing workspace, view schedules and customer activity, review campaign results, and use the tools available to their account. Workspace permissions keep responsibilities clear for team members.

Scalers can manage their profile and service areas, browse available opportunities, follow assigned work, review submitted work and see authoritative earnings information. Work availability depends on the market and account eligibility.

Notifications help you return to relevant work, account updates and review items. You control optional notification preferences. Location is used during an active tracked assignment; location supports route evidence during an active tracked assignment.
