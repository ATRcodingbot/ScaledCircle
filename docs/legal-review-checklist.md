# ScaledCircle Legal Review Checklist

Status: engineering/product-truth draft for professional review. This document is not legal advice and does not mark public staging language attorney-approved.

## Product-verified facts

- ScaledCircle is the product brand; Scaled Circle LLC is the operating legal entity; `support@scaledcircle.com` is the approved public support channel.
- Businesses fund campaigns in real currency through Stripe. Business credits are retired.
- The reviewed field-campaign platform fee is 20% of worker compensation. The reviewed direct-mail/postcard fee is 20%, with printing, postage, and vendor costs separate. Social advertising spend has a 0% percentage markup.
- Eligible unassigned, unstarted funded campaigns can use the maintained full-refund lifecycle only after server eligibility checks. Assignment/worker obligation blocks instant self-service cancellation. Ordinary Business cancellation must not erase verified worker earnings.
- Scalers choose whether to apply; application does not guarantee assignment. Assignment locks compensation. Location/evidence can be required. Submission alone does not establish earning; approved server-authoritative verification is the reviewed future boundary. Provider payout is separate and not production-launch certified.
- Android active-job tracking uses a foreground service and visible notification and does not request `ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION`. The maintained photo flow uses the system/external picker/capture integration and does not require a permanent CAMERA manifest permission.
- Transactional messages are separate from optional marketing. Autonomous outbound is not implemented. Sales suppression is structured.
- Founder policy requires every account holder to be at least 18. A Business account holder must also be authorized to act for and bind the represented Business. Exact wording and enforcement remain counsel-review items before official broad public launch.

## Professional legal review required

- Scaler worker classification and the complete independent-contractor agreement.
- Age/minimum eligibility, capacity to contract, and any guardian/minor restrictions.
- Governing law, venue, dispute resolution, arbitration, class-action waiver, limitations of liability, warranty disclaimers, and indemnification.
- Tax reporting, KYC, payout responsibility, withholding, and worker/affiliate documentation.
- Precise retention periods for location routes, photos/evidence, support, Sales, campaign, and financial/audit records.
- Jurisdiction-specific privacy rights, request verification, deletion exceptions, appeal process, and target-market cookie/tracker consent requirements.
- Marketing/outreach, telephone, email, social, and prospecting compliance by target jurisdiction.
- Affiliate compensation, reversal, eligibility, suspension, tax, accounting, and future settlement terms.
- Refund/dispute wording, processing-cost treatment, chargebacks, administrative exceptions, and any state-specific cancellation rights.
- Postal/direct-mail responsibilities, vendor terms, claims, and prohibited content.

## Release rule

Founder product policy is approved for the engineering release. Production pages must remain labeled as product-grounded policy—not attorney-approved or legally certified. Professional review of the listed binding terms remains an explicit gate before official broad public launch. Product facts must not be changed to make legal drafting easier.
