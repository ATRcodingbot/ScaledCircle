# First genuine Maryland paid campaign — Admin operating checklist

Prepared September 26, 2026. Read-only checks first. This checklist grants no payment, assignment, refund, payout or override authority. Use maintained Admin actions and record the campaign, provider-operation and ledger references in the existing audit history. Never use the 500-material QA draft as a real order.

## Before accepting a campaign

- Confirm the real Business owner, Maryland territory, actual materials, scope, dates and accepted quote. Core membership is separate from campaign funding. Supported types are Flyer Distribution, Door Hanger Distribution and Door-to-Door Outreach; reject unsupported types before charge.
- Confirm the maintained server eligibility response is fresh and belongs to this campaign/current account. Missing, expired or unavailable evidence means STOP and refresh/reconcile; a redirect, funded badge or pooled Stripe balance is not authority.
- Confirm signed provider payment/subscription reconciliation and the exact campaign allocation. Check fees and all protected commitments. Do not count the same customer payment twice or assume contingency cash exists.
- Verify the immutable accepted compensation contract: worker identity, base, maximum accepted bonus, scope and contract version. Reserved compensation must cover the maintained maximum obligation, including other campaigns' protections.
- Verify the real assignment and acceptance, current required Business/Scaler consent, supported geography/type, valid work window, material readiness and required route evidence. Re-read the authoritative work-start gate immediately before work starts.
- Confirm no unresolved refund, dispute, uncertain provider operation or funding deficit blocks this campaign. STOP if reserve/backing cannot be proved. Never substitute an Admin statement for the actual start gate.

## During work and review

- Follow the existing tracking/session lifecycle. Retain authentic route and completion evidence. Route proximity is not proof of delivery to every household or guaranteed conversations/leads/sales.
- On submission, use the maintained finalization and completion review. STOP for incomplete evidence, changed contract, stale/invalid session, technical review or access exception requiring review. Do not fabricate progress or manually mark a test attended/completed.
- Apply the accepted compensation policy through the maintained exactly-once earning action. Record earning and worker-obligation references. Distinguish reserved, earned/unpaid and paid; provider acceptance alone is not recipient bank receipt.
- A later cancellation/refund/dispute does not erase earned compensation. Preserve the obligation and escalate a funding issue through the audited path; do not delay already-owed compensation as a new operating rule.

## Refunds, disputes and withdrawal

- Use the existing logical refund operation/idempotency key. Atomic platform capacity reservation must protect other campaigns' reserved and earned unpaid compensation, pending refunds, uncertain attempts, fees and other commitments. STOP if capacity is insufficient or authority unavailable.
- Timeout/unknown provider result: retain the reservation and reconcile before retrying. Never create a second refund or release capacity because the first response was lost. Duplicate success must not duplicate ledger effects.
- Replacement-funding recovery remains inactive until actual verified replacement funds and separate explicit financial authorization exist. No assumed top-up, personal guarantee or future revenue.
- Keep platform payouts manual. Before any separately authorized withdrawal, use the current authoritative safe-withdrawal calculation. Exclude every protected balance; missing/uncertain authority means $0. Do not treat a displayed balance as permission to withdraw.
- STOP on cross-campaign backing loss, negative/unreconciled capacity, duplicate operations or mismatch between provider and ledger. Preserve evidence and existing obligations; do not bypass controls or change payout settings.

## Closeout

Record reconciled customer payment, approved completion evidence, immutable contract, exactly-once earning, outstanding/paid worker obligation and any unresolved refund/dispute. A campaign is not financially closed merely because the UI says Completed. No automatic expansion of the pilot or economic authority follows from this checklist.
