# Future lead-to-calendar scheduling

This requirement is recorded for later implementation. It is not part of the current referral and email-campaign release.

After a prospect is qualified, the agent may offer actual Business schedule availability using appointment duration, travel time, service geography, conflicts, and Business-defined user/crew skills and assignment preferences. Prefer practical same-day, same-area grouping without offering an occupied or infeasible window. The Business controls which people or crews handle estimates, project work and phone consultations.

An offered appointment window is not a confirmed appointment. Confirmation requires the prospect's agreement and an authoritative final conflict/assignment check.

Active prospecting, qualification and autonomous booking belong to the Lead Generation add-on ($699/month) and are included in Growth Department ($2,000/month). They must not be silently included in Managed Growth ($999/month). Core Schedule may supply assignment rules and scheduling intelligence. No pricing or entitlement change is authorized by recording this requirement.
