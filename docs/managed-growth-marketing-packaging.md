# Managed Growth marketing packaging

Canonical launch packaging:

| Plan | Monthly price | Total seats | Included Social / Email Campaigns | Generated source concepts/month |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|
| Starter | $99 | 1 | No | 0 |
| Growth | $299 | 3 | No | 0 |
| Scale | $499 | 5 | No | 0 |
| Managed Growth | $999 | 10 | Yes, Private Beta / Invite Only | Up to 60 |
| Growth Department | $2,000 | 10 | Inherits Managed Growth once | Up to 60 |

Core Business OS remains available on all paid workspace plans. Business Assistant (+$399)
and Lead Generation (+$699) remain separate products; Growth Department includes both.
Managed Growth does not grant either add-on by itself.

Social launch channels are Facebook and Instagram. X remains gated; YouTube remains Coming Soon.
Provider capability never grants content approval. Existing owner approval, safety checks,
invitation, provider-health and scheduling controls remain required.

Generation counts source concepts, not Facebook/Instagram derivatives. Real Business photos,
intentional text-only posts and reuse consume no generation units. Existing provider accounting,
unknown-result reservations and per-day cost/rate safeguards remain independent.

Email Campaign execution requires active Managed Growth and the existing private invitation.
Delivery workers recheck this authority before sending. Ordinary mailbox access, transactional
account communications and suppression controls are not converted into marketing entitlements.
Existing explicit internal Founder dogfood authority remains distinct from included customer plans.

Growth Department's maintained subscription selection resolves to Managed Growth plus its bundle
entitlements; no duplicate subscription item or second generation allowance is introduced.
This correction creates no provider objects, sends no messages and changes no customer subscription.
