# Referral launch authority

Founder-approved policy: Business referrals receive 10% of qualifying retained recurring ScaledCircle subscription revenue. Scaler referrals receive 1% of final approved Scaler compensation from qualifying completed jobs, including earned bonuses and excluding fees, taxes and returned reserves. Both are single-level. No downline rewards or automatic rate progression.

The Scaler reward is funded separately by ScaledCircle platform economics. This does not come out of the Scaler's pay. Accepted base, earned bonus, immutable compensation, worker Wallet credit and Business-authorized charges remain unchanged. Amounts round to the nearest cent using integer arithmetic; $100 qualifying compensation produces a separate $1 reward. Inadequate platform economics or conflicting worker-pay records fail closed.

## Existing authority inspected

The existing affiliate service supplied approved-Scaler enrollment, opaque codes, a 30-day Business attribution window, a 10% default and an older Admin 10-30% range. Accounting explicitly reported unavailable; there was no invoice commission, reversal or referral payout ledger. No previous Scaler-referral monetary rule existed. The Founder has now supplied that rule. New Business rate changes are capped at 10%; older records are preserved and flagged for review.

## Staging implementation

Approved Scalers and authenticated Business workspace owners can explicitly accept Referral Launch V2 terms, generate links/QRs, and view separate Businesses Referred and Scalers Referred histories. Business ownership is independently checked by the maintained workspace authority. Existing general legal consent records are not changed. Referral policy acceptance is recorded separately. No actual person's enrollment or acceptance is fabricated.

Attribution is immutable first-touch, authenticated, self-referral protected and private. Signup produces a deterministic in-app New Referral notification and no money. Scaler attribution is limited to new accounts without previous paid-work records. Contact information is not exposed to the referrer.

A separate staging reward reconciler rereads settlement, final review, immutable compensation, transfer reservation, signed funding and referral records in a transaction. It does not trust event payload amounts. It requires ordinary approved completed work after attribution. Submitted, canceled, disputed, reversed, refunded or mismatched work fails closed. Certified unused-reserve returns are distinguished from refunds of earned work; ambiguous/shared partial refunds remain held for review.

Each qualifying job creates one deterministic EARNED liability and a balanced journal: debit platform referral expense, credit referral held liability. There is no worker or Business debit. Duplicate events create no duplicate liability or notification. Loss of eligibility reverses the held liability once and preserves the journal. No historical replay or QA fixture mutation is performed.

## States and release boundaries

- SIGNED_UP: referral relationship only, no money.
- EARNED / Earning: qualifying economic event creates a held reward.
- AVAILABLE: requires a separately verified release authority; unavailable in this release.
- PAID: requires confirmed disbursement; unavailable in this release.

The public-page copy, portal and program FAQ explicitly protect Scaler pay. No payout date or guaranteed income is promised. Business subscription reward accounting remains inactive until retained-revenue eligibility, refund/chargeback adjustments and release authority are certified. Referral payout execution is not implemented or activated by this change. No fake available/paid notification is sent.

All new triggers and owner-portal endpoints fail closed outside staging. Production referral enrollment remains Coming Soon. This change does not deploy production, create Stripe objects, alter subscription membership, post worker earnings, change Wallets, or modify historical QA evidence.
