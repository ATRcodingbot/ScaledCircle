# Referral production Private Beta

The production launch entry is `functions/referral_launch_entry.js`, prepared by `functions/scripts/prepare_referral_launch.js`. Its exact selector manifest replaces the five legacy affiliate endpoints and supplies current portal, attribution, financial read-model, Admin review and reconciliation endpoints. Use a bounded `platform-core` deployment selection; never deploy the shared Functions export inventory. The original shared staging payout entry is not a production promotion candidate.

The policy is 10% of qualifying retained recurring subscription revenue and 1% of final approved completed-work compensation. Rewards are platform-funded, single-level liabilities. No worker compensation, bonus, Wallet, campaign reserve, payment or subscription record is written by the launch authority. Signup creates attribution and one notification, never money. Referral terms require the user's real authenticated acceptance. Business participation requires workspace ownership; Scaler participation requires approval and verified email.

Production reconciliation consumes the existing signed `stripeCampaignEvents` and `subscriptionPaymentReceipts` records, then rereads the matching LIVE provider event, canonical prices, invoice payments, charges, refunds, credit notes and disputes. It does not replace the subscription webhook. TEST evidence is rejected. Recorded economic snapshots are checked again in the ledger transaction. Unknown allocations are held for review. Ongoing provider rechecks catch retained-revenue changes without changing the webhook event union.

The production provider facade exposes only required reads. The existing TEST Connect execution runtime keeps its staging-only assertions. Production liabilities require the explicit `ReferralPrivateBetaManualV1` policy and exact production environment. Automatic referral release, transfer, cashout and payout are not enabled.

Customer states are Pending and Under Review until payment authority actually supports something else. The 30-day Business and 7-day Scaler windows indicate the earliest economic review, not promised availability or scheduled payment. The Admin review operation rereads economics, verifies a current digest and amount, and stores an immutable actor/reason/source review. It does not edit amounts or declare a payment. Actual first payment requires an attended financial checkpoint with a verified recipient and certified provider operation; manual payment never means editing ledger numbers. Existing paid receipts and reversal journals remain intact.

Production `stripeWebhook`, subscription/intro-offer behavior, state rollout, worker Wallets and campaign authorities are KEEP CURRENT. The narrow client overlay exposes the referral portal and Admin review and uses the maintained signup attribution path. Rollback restores the prior Hosting release and captured legacy function archives; it never deletes referral evidence created after launch.
